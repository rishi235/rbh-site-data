THE DEFECT: THE QUALIFIERS ARE GUARDED BY NOTHING
Every advertising rule in the estate bans wording. None requires any. The two
private clinics are sold on qualified offers, and the qualifier is the part
that makes the advertisement lawful. Six edits walked past ALL 36 CHECKERS:

  UNCAUGHT  delete "This is a private, paid service and it is not right for
            everyone - the pharmacist will advise." from Post C
  UNCAUGHT  delete "as part of a supervised plan alongside diet and lifestyle
            changes" from Post C
  UNCAUGHT  delete "as part of a supervised plan" from the services weight
            loss bullet
  UNCAUGHT  delete "subject to availability and clinical suitability" from
            Post D
  UNCAUGHT  delete the same wording from the services travel bullet
  UNCAUGHT  change "private, paid service" to "free service", which advertises
            a paid weight loss clinic as free on a public Google profile

A GBP post is Regime 1 under the house weight loss standard, the stricter
half: an advertisement pushed onto a public profile, not an inner page the
patient chose to open. The estate already bans the medicine names and the
efficacy claims there. It did not hold the qualifier sitting beside them.

WHY NOTHING SAW IT
check-weight-loss-copy.js reads modules/service/pages, modules/branch/pages
and modules/service/weebly-paste. It does not read gbp-packs/ at all, so the
weight loss checker has never read a pack. check-gbp-packs.js carries
MEDICINE_NAMES, EFFICACY_FAIL and the shared CLAIM_PATTERNS, all of them
prohibitions. And gbp-packs/TEMPLATE.md tells the drafter "no medicine names,
no efficacy claims" for Post C and says nothing at all about the qualifiers.
So the convention was kept by twelve packs and asked for by no file.

ONE RESIDUAL, STATED NOT FIXED
An outcome claim outside the known vocabulary ("we get results for everyone")
still passes. check-gbp-packs.js already sets out, at its claims-outside-the-
known-vocabulary note, why a list-based claim detector can only catch a claim
it has a name for. That is a standing design position, not a new finding, and
widening it is a larger question than this pass.

THE FIX, IN THE CHECKER AND THE TEMPLATE, NOT IN ANY PUBLISHED COPY
tools/check-gbp-packs.js: new CLINIC_QUALIFIERS rule, six markers, each gated
on the branch's own widget set and on the pack actually having that post, so
no branch is asked for a qualifier on a service it does not run. Composed from
branches.json and the pack's own sections; nothing hardcoded per branch.
Wording is matched loosely enough to survive an honest rewrite: "not right for
everyone" and "not suitable for everyone" both count, and the
pharmacist-will-advise clause satisfies that requirement on its own.
gbp-packs/TEMPLATE.md: sections 3 and 5 now tell the drafter to write the
qualifiers, because a rule enforced in a checker and absent from the template
it is drafted from is the drift the item 4.1 pass caught on 2026-08-13.
