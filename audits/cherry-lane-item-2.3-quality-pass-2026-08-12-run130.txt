ONE MISS, and it was the sharp one. The Pharmacy First link on a Cherry Lane
service page was repointed at pharmacy-first-coleman-leigh-walton.html, the
other Walton branch, and all 29 checkers exited 0. Rule 9 SISTERLINK, also
added yesterday, covers the branch landing family only and says so in terms:
its comment reads "so pharmacy-first-fishlocks-ainsdale.html is not mistaken
for one". Every other page family - pharmacy-first, the six Pharmacy First
condition pages, uti, contraception, travel clinic, weight loss, switch - was
unchecked. A patient on the Cherry Lane UTI page clicking through to book
Pharmacy First could be sent to a different pharmacy, and nothing in the repo
would say so. Cherry Lane and Coleman and Leighs are both in Walton, both in
L4, about a mile apart, which is what makes the wrong link plausible rather
than obviously absurd.

THE FIX. tools/check-branch-identity.js gains rule 10 SERVICELINK: a link to a
generated service page (<service>-<brandSlug>-<townSlug>.html) must point at
the page's own branch. The owning branch is read off the filename by longest
matching brandSlug-townSlug suffix, longest so that one branch key ending
inside another resolves to the more specific branch. Landing links are skipped
so rule 9 keeps sole ownership of that family and nothing is reported twice.
The failure message distinguishes the two harms, because they are not the same
fault: where the branches share a host the link resolves and the patient is
quietly booked into the wrong pharmacy, and where they do not it is a relative
link to another domain, so it 404s and the service route is simply dead.

NEGATIVE TESTED, AND ON THE CASE THAT MATTERS MOST. On the clean tree the
rule reports nothing and reads 238 service page links across the 177 pages, so
it has real coverage and no false positives. Re-injected, it catches the
original Cherry Lane miss. It also catches the same fault on the Fishlocks
shared domain, where Ainsdale was pointed at Eccleston's Pharmacy First page -
same website, so the link resolves and the misbooking is silent. That is item
2.2's whole harm, and until this run nothing tested for it.

The pattern is now the same one recorded three times in CLAUDE.md and twice in
this log: the checker that owns a field reads only branches.json, and the
checkers that read pages do not own the field. Rules 8, 9 and 10 have each
closed one instance of it.
