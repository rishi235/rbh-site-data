NEW GENUINE FINDING (not fixed this pass - see QUESTIONS.json)
----------------------------------------------------------------
tools/pom-names.js's PHARMACY_FIRST group (the list rule 8 of this checker,
and every other checker requiring pom-names.js, reads) holds only the
GENERIC name for each of the eight medicines the seven NHS Pharmacy First
pathways prescribe from: nitrofurantoin, amoxicillin,
phenoxymethylpenicillin, clarithromycin, aciclovir, fusidic,
flucloxacillin, hydrocortisone. It holds no UK brand-name equivalent for
any of them, unlike the WEIGHT_LOSS and TRAVEL_VACCINES groups in the same
file, which are predominantly BRAND names (Mounjaro, Wegovy, Stamaril,
Zostavax...). Proven above: "aciclovir" is caught by rule 8, the
brand-equivalent "Zovirax" is not, on an otherwise identical injected
sentence. The gap is real today only in the sense that nothing in the
generator currently types a medicine name anywhere in symptom copy (the
generator's own committed symptoms arrays are clean and have been for
every one of item 3.5's twenty prior passes), so there is no live
exposure - but the checker's own stated job ("a symptom point may not name
a medicine from tools/pom-names.js") is a narrower guarantee than its own
one-line description implies while the list is generic-only, and a hand
edit naming a brand would currently pass silently. Raised as Q110 rather
than fixed in this pass: getting a UK medicine brand-name list right is a
clinical fact, not an editorial judgement call, and pom-names.js's own
header is explicit that names should not be added or removed without
recording the decision. Recommended candidates for Rishi or the
superintendent pharmacist to confirm before adding: Zovirax (aciclovir),
Fucidin (fusidic acid), Macrobid and Macrodantin (nitrofurantoin), Klaricid
(clarithromycin) - the four I am confident are current UK-marketed
products for these exact indications. Deliberately not proposing brand
names for amoxicillin, phenoxymethylpenicillin, flucloxacillin or
hydrocortisone, since I am not confident which (if any) branded products
remain current or in what strength/combination, and a wrong or
discontinued brand name in a compliance word list is worse than a
documented gap.
