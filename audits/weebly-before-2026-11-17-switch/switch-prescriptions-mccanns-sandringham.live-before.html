How switching to McCanns Chemist works

Most people put off switching pharmacy because they assume it will be a hassle. With McCanns Chemist in Sandringham, it usually is not — we make the first step quick and easy.

1

Fill in the form

Enter your first name, last name and date of birth. Mobile and email are optional.

2

We review your request

3

Collect from us

Your prescriptions come to McCanns Chemist and you get support from a real local pharmacy team.

Questions people usually ask

Is switching pharmacy to McCanns Chemist a hassle?
Usually no. Most people delay it because they assume it will be awkward, but switching to McCanns Chemist in Sandringham is much simpler than expected.
Do I need to contact my GP myself?
Not always. We help guide the process and handle what we can from our side. If anything extra is needed, we will tell you clearly.
Why do you only ask for basic details?
Because long forms put people off. For the first step, we only need enough information to identify you and start the process properly.
What if I am not ready yet?
Call us on 0151 727 3076, request a callback, or send a WhatsApp message instead. The aim is to make this easy, not pushy.
