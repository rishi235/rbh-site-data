FIX APPLIED
-----------
tools/check-pharmacy-first-eligibility.js, rule 5: changed the heading and
cohort-line checks from `text.indexOf(...)` (flat whole-page text) to
`segments.indexOf(...)` (exact match against one block-level segment, the
same `visibleSegments()` machinery rule 7 already uses). A segment is scoped
to one block element (li, p, h1-6, div, section, td, th, dd, dt) or a <br>
break, so the eligibility <li> and the hero pill (an inline <span> that
bleeds into its surrounding block) can never be the same segment, and an
exact match against the <li>'s own segment cannot be satisfied by the pill
sitting elsewhere on the page.
