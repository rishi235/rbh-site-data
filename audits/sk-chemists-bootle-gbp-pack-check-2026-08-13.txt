THE ONE REAL DEFECT FOUND, AND FIXED IN tools/check-pharmacy-first-eligibility.js
  Deleting the sentence "Age ranges set by the NHS apply to each
  condition." from Post A, and changing nothing else, walked past all 31
  checkers clean.

  Why it matters. A pack prints an age for one pathway out of seven, the
  UTI cohort in Post A, plus the blood pressure cohort in the business
  description. The other six pathways carry NHS age limits the pack never
  names: sinusitis 12 and over, sore throat 5 and over, earache 1 to 17,
  impetigo 1 and over, shingles 18 and over. Rule 9 pins the ages a pack
  states and rule 10 pins the list it prints, so a pack could satisfy both
  and still publish a flat seven-condition offer with nothing saying who
  is eligible. That is a parent bringing a six-month-old for the earache
  pathway that starts at 1, or a sore throat consultation asked for on a
  four-year-old when the pathway starts at 5, and being turned away at the
  counter after reading the profile.

  Why this was a fix and not a question. It pins a convention the estate
  already keeps rather than making a new decision. All 14 packs that
  enumerate the conditions carry the sentence: cherry-lane-walton,
  coleman-leigh-walton, fishlocks-ainsdale, fishlocks-eccleston,
  gordon-short-crosby, hirshmans-ainsdale, mccanns-aigburth,
  mccanns-sandringham, riddings-timperley, scorah-bramhall,
  scorah-hazel-grove, sk-chemists-bootle, smartts-bootle and
  tiffenbergs-aintree. The two that name no conditions, clear-aintree.md
  and TEMPLATE.md, make no claim to qualify and are exempt. No pack copy,
  no page, no generator, no data field and no patient-facing wording was
  changed by this fix.

  The rule is tied to enumeration rather than to Post A by name, because
  the enumeration is what creates the duty to qualify and it is not always
  Post A that carries it. The matcher is whitespace-insensitive because
  the packs wrap the sentence across a line break, and a line-bound match
  reads a wrapped sentence as absent.

  Proved after the fix: deleting the sentence now fails
  check-pharmacy-first-eligibility.js on sk-chemists-bootle.md,
  tiffenbergs-aintree.md, scorah-bramhall.md and cherry-lane-walton.md,
  and all 31 checkers are green again once restored.
