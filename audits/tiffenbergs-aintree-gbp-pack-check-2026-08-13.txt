THE DEFECT THIS PASS FOUND: NOTHING READ THE COST CLAIM
The previous run left three verifier gaps for the next passes and named this
one first. It is real and it is estate-wide.

NHS Pharmacy First is free at the point of use. The consultation costs the
patient nothing, and if a medicine is supplied the usual NHS prescription
charge applies unless they are exempt. Those two sentences are the whole cost
claim, published on 112 generated pages and in 14 GBP packs. No checker read a
word of it. Every existing Pharmacy First rule guards WHO the service is for
(check-pharmacy-first-eligibility.js, cohorts) or WHERE an excluded patient
goes (check-pharmacy-first-safety-net.js, onward routes). Neither touches cost,
and the SEO checkers read the shape of a string, not its meaning.

The asymmetry is the point: check-contraception-copy.js rule 5 has required the
no-charge answer and failed a stated price on every NHS contraception page
since it was written. NHS contraception and NHS Pharmacy First are commissioned
the same way and free the same way, and only one of them was guarded.

PROVED BY INJECTION BEFORE THE FIX
On modules/service/pages/pharmacy-first-tiffenbergs-aintree.html, the hero pill
changed from "Free NHS service at your local Aintree pharmacy" to "Low-cost NHS
service at your local Aintree pharmacy" walked past ALL 31 CHECKERS CLEAN. That
is a free NHS service advertised as a paid one on the page a patient reads
before deciding whether they can afford to be seen. The harm runs the quiet
way: nobody complains about a page that wrongly implies a fee, they simply do
not come, and the pharmacy never learns why. The opposite direction is worse
for the patient who does come - dropping the prescription-charge caveat leaves
the page promising something free and the counter asking for a charge nobody
mentioned.

THE FIX: tools/check-pharmacy-first-cost.js (new, 6 rules)
  Rule 1 source   - the generator still composes both halves of the claim, and
                    the seven pathway keys this checker matches filenames on
                    have not drifted from the generator's own. A floor on the
                    parser, so rules 2 and 3 cannot pass while reading nothing.
  Rule 2 free     - every Pharmacy First page states the service is a free NHS
                    one.
  Rule 3 caveat   - every Pharmacy First page keeps the NHS prescription-charge
                    sentence. Overclaiming is not the safe direction.
  Rule 4 no cost qualifier - no page or pack describes the service as low-cost,
                    affordable, cheap, discounted, great value, a small fee, a
                    consultation fee or payable. Phrases, not words: the pages
                    correctly say "no charge to be seen" and "the usual NHS
                    prescription charge applies", so a word-level list would
                    fail the copy it exists to protect.
  Rule 5 no price - no page or pack states a currency amount or a prices-from
                    line on Pharmacy First copy.
  Rule 6 pack coverage - a pack whose branch runs Pharmacy First calls the
                    service free in a sentence that names the service.

Scoping. The 112 pages are scanned whole, because a Pharmacy First page is
about nothing else. A pack is not: the same file advertises a private weight
loss clinic and a private travel clinic, and those are allowed to carry a
price. Packs are therefore scoped by block, and within a Pharmacy First block a
sentence plainly about a private service that does not name Pharmacy First is
left alone.
