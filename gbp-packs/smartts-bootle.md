## 1. Business description (max 750 chars - this is 710)
Smartts Chemist is an independent NHS pharmacy at 42 Fernhill Road in
Bootle, serving Bootle, Sefton and Liverpool. We dispense NHS
prescriptions, provide free NHS blood pressure checks for adults aged 40
and over, and offer the NHS contraception service. Through the free NHS
Pharmacy First service our pharmacists can assess and treat seven common
conditions with no GP appointment needed. Private services include a
travel clinic, a pharmacist-led weight loss clinic, blood tests,
vaccinations and a free consultation to discuss eligibility for medical
cannabis treatment. You can order repeat prescriptions through our app.
Open Monday to Friday, closed 1pm to 2pm for lunch. Call in and speak to
the team.

## 3. Services section content
NHS services first:
- NHS prescription dispensing: prescriptions dispensed by a local team on
  Fernhill Road, serving Bootle, Sefton and Liverpool, with repeat
  ordering through the Smartts app.
- NHS Pharmacy First: free NHS assessment and, where appropriate,
  treatment for sinusitis, sore throat, earache, impetigo, shingles,
  infected insect bites and uncomplicated UTIs in women, with no GP
  appointment needed.
- NHS blood pressure check: free blood pressure checks for adults aged 40
  and over, no appointment needed.
- NHS contraception service: start or continue oral contraception at the
  pharmacy without a GP appointment.
Private services:
- Travel clinic: private travel health consultation covering your
  destination, itinerary and medical history, with vaccinations and
  malaria prevention advice, subject to availability and clinical
  suitability.
- Weight loss clinic: pharmacist-led private consultations with a full
  clinical assessment and ongoing support as part of a supervised plan.
- Blood tests: private blood testing at the pharmacy; see the website for
  the tests currently offered.
- Vaccinations: private vaccination service; ask the team or see the
  website for what is currently available.
- Medical cannabis consultation: book a free consultation to discuss
  eligibility.
- Private consultation room: confidential advice on your medicines.

### Post A - Pharmacy First
See a pharmacist, not a waiting list. Pharmacy First is a free NHS service
at Smartts Chemist on Fernhill Road in Bootle. Our pharmacists can assess
and, where appropriate, treat seven common conditions: sinusitis, sore
throat, earache in children, impetigo, shingles, infected insect bites and
uncomplicated water infections in women aged 16 to 64. No GP appointment
or referral needed - book or just walk in. Age ranges set by the NHS apply
to each condition.

### Post B - Switch your prescriptions
Switching your prescriptions to Smartts Chemist in Bootle takes about 30
seconds. Tell us your name, date of birth and GP surgery - we contact the
surgery and handle everything else. Order repeats through our app and
collect from a local team on Fernhill Road you can actually speak to.
Serving Bootle, Sefton and Liverpool.
Button: Learn more -> https://www.smarttschemist.co.uk/switch-prescriptions-smartts-bootle.html
