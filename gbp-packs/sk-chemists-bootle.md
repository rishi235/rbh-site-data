## 1. Business description (max 750 chars - this is 735)
SK Chemists is a friendly independent NHS pharmacy at 516 Stanley Road,
Bootle, looking after patients across Bootle, Sefton and the wider
Liverpool area. As well as dispensing NHS prescriptions, we take part in
Pharmacy First, the free NHS service that lets a pharmacist assess and,
where appropriate, treat seven common conditions without a GP appointment.
We also offer free NHS blood pressure checks for anyone aged 40 or over
and the NHS contraception service. Our private clinics cover travel
health, with vaccination and malaria prevention advice tailored to your
trip, and a pharmacist-led weight loss clinic built around a full clinical
assessment and ongoing support. Open 9am to 6pm Monday to Friday. Come in
and talk to us.

## 3. Services section content
NHS services first:
- NHS prescription dispensing: NHS prescriptions dispensed on Stanley Road
  by a team that knows its regulars, serving Bootle, Sefton and Liverpool.
- NHS Pharmacy First: free NHS assessment and, where appropriate,
  treatment for sinusitis, sore throat, earache, impetigo, shingles,
  infected insect bites and uncomplicated UTIs in women, with no GP
  appointment needed.
- NHS blood pressure check: free blood pressure checks for adults aged 40
  and over, no appointment needed.
- NHS contraception service: start or continue oral contraception at the
  pharmacy without a GP appointment.
Private services:
- Travel clinic: private travel health consultation covering your
  destination, itinerary and medical history, with vaccinations and
  malaria prevention advice, subject to availability and clinical
  suitability.
- Weight loss clinic: pharmacist-led private consultations with a full
  clinical assessment and ongoing support as part of a supervised plan.
- Private consultation room: confidential advice on your medicines.
