THE DEFECT

(line 145), Page Permalink (146) and Page Description (147). Weebly's SEO panel
has FOUR fields. It never parses Meta Keywords, so that field is compared to
nothing there - 177 lines of public copy across 11 sheets.

That field does have a dedicated guard, check-seo-keywords.js, with five rules
on its content. The one that would catch a wrong town is RULE 4: "the keywords
name ANOTHER LIVE BRANCH's seoTown". Its edge is therefore the set of live
seoTowns. Item 5.7 RETIRED a town word. A retired word belongs to no branch, so
it is not in that set, and nothing holds it.

THE FIX
tools/check-seo-keywords.js, RULE 8 - retired town word. When a branch's
townSlug, de-slugged, is no longer its seoTown, that word is retired; it may
stay in the permalink, but it may not appear in the pasted keywords unless it is
genuinely in the branch's own serviceAreaList. Derived from data already in
branches.json, so it needs no list of retired words to maintain and covers the
next townSlug hold automatically. Exactly one branch in the estate diverges
today, which is 5.7's own; the other 14 skip. A normTown() helper compares a
slug to a town name without a slugify dependency and without tripping on the
ASCII apostrophe 5.7 deliberately kept. The run summary now states how many
branches the rule is actually guarding, so it cannot quietly cover nothing.
