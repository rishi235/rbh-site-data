WHY THIS CHECKER, WHY NOW
--------------------------

no sister on cherrylanepharmacy.co.uk), so rules 4/5/6/9/11 of

under item 2.3's own quality-pass log (2026-08-12), not item 3.4's - this
repo's convention (see item 2.1's twenty-first pass) is that each worklist
item tracks its own checker-coverage count independently, so it is retested
and logged here.
