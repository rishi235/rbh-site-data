WHY THIS IS THE STRICTER HALF, NOT THE LOOSER ONE
  AI\RBH_WeightLoss_Advertising_Standards.md splits by how a page is REACHED,
  not by how important it looks. The inner-page exemption covers "the inner
  pages of a clinic or pharmacy's own website, but not the homepage or a
  proactively linked landing page".

  A branch landing page is a proactively linked landing page by construction:
  each of the six packs in gbp-packs/ sets a Google Business Profile website
  field to one of these six URLs. So the 15 weight loss pages, which a
  consumer chooses to open, sit in the looser regime and were fully checked;
  the six landing pages sit in the near-total prohibition and were checked by
  nothing.

RULE 11, ADDED
  Whole page, comments blanked, RAW rather than visible text, because the
  standard bars a POM reference in "even hover text and small print":
    - no name from pom-names.WEIGHT_LOSS (21 names)
    - no POM class reference: GLP-1, skinny jab, fat jab, obesity treatment
      jab, weight loss / slimming injections, jabs, pens or shots
    - no "Buy Now", "Add to Basket", "Buy [product]"
  Weight loss sentences only, so Pharmacy First and travel clinic copy on the
  same page is not held to a rule written for another service:
    - no once-a-week or weekly-injectable dosing reference
    - no rate-of-loss or body-part claim (CAP rule 13.9)
    - no offer, discount, half price, Black Friday, money off, free medicine
    - no efficacy or results claim, from tools/claim-patterns.js
  Positive floor, anchored on the tile that links into the weight loss page:
    - that tile must call the service a consultation
  Floors:
    - source floor on tools/build-branch-landing-pages.js: if it composes no
      weight loss wording at all, rule 11 fails rather than passing on nothing
    - coverage floor of four pages, same figure and reason as rule 7 of
      check-pharmacy-first-cost.js

  DELIBERATELY NOT BARRED: a plain consultation price. The same standard
  permits "only indicative prices for a condition ... e.g. consultation price
  and initial course price" on a homepage-class page. Failing that would be
  the checker inventing a rule. What is barred is the offer wrapped round the
  price, which is what the ASA rulings are actually about.
