FINDING (injection 4)
The "anywhere in the pack" rule splits each sentence into comma/semicolon/
colon-bounded segments and, per segment, requires BOTH a day word and an hours
or closure claim to be present before it will test anything - a segment with a
closure claim and no day word of its own is silently skipped. Tiffenbergs' own
business description states its lunch closure in exactly that shape: the day
clause ("Open Monday to Friday") and the closure clause ("closed 1pm to 2pm for
lunch") sit in two different comma-separated segments of the same sentence, so
the closure segment carries no day word and the rule never reaches it. This is
not unique to Tiffenbergs: grepping every gbp-packs/*.md for "lunch" found the
identical construction, word for word ("Open Monday to Friday, closed 1pm to
2pm for lunch."), in coleman-leigh-walton.md and smartts-bootle.md. Both branches
were confirmed via branches.json to be genuine split-day (lunch-closure)
branches with a 13:00-14:00 gap on Monday to Friday, and both packs currently
state that gap correctly - re-read directly, not assumed - so this closes a
latent hole across three packs rather than a live breach on any of them.
Gordon Short Crosby's pack states the same fact with no comma between the day
clause and the closure clause ("The pharmacy closes for lunch 1:00pm to 2:00pm
Monday to Saturday"), which is why the item 4.14 pass that built this rule never
tripped over the gap: the sentence shape it was proved against does not have it.

FIX
tools/check-gbp-packs.js, the "an hours statement ANYWHERE in the pack" rule
(the segment loop under the "outside the guarded hours line" comment block).
Added a sentence-scoped carryDays variable: a segment that names no day of its
own now inherits the day set from the most recent segment in the SAME sentence
that did name one, rather than being skipped for want of a day word. carryDays
is reset for every sentence and is only ever set from a segment's own days,
never from an inherited value, so it cannot chain a day claim across a sentence
boundary or manufacture a day nobody stated. Comment added in place explaining
the defect, the fix, and the two other affected packs, in the file's existing
narrative style.
