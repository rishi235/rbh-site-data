  check-nap caught that one.
- Root cause: every rule in check-postcodes reads postcodes through
  extract(), and neither PC_RE nor PC_RE_LOOSE could read a postcode
  whose separator is %20, %2B, a literal + or a hyphen. A postcode
  travels inside links as well as prose, and the packs (and any future
  URL-carrying surface outside the three generated page dirs) had no
  other checker behind them.

THE FIX
- PC_RE_LOOSE separator widened to add %20, %2B, + and hyphen, in the
  one extract() both scan sites share. Still INTEREST-bounded: a loose
  match only counts if it canonicalises to a postcode the repo has a
  position on, so date ranges, CSS tokens and street-number ranges
  like 61-63 stay unread.
