Gordon Short Chemist

Your local community pharmacy
Welcome to Gordon Short Chemist!

Located in Crosby, Liverpool, we have proudly served our local community since 1960. Our commitment to personal service and attention to detail sets us apart, ensuring that every visitor receives the care and respect they deserve.

Our Services Include:
  • NHS Prescriptions: Convenient dispensing service with both while-you-wait and free home delivery options. We can pick up prescriptions from your GP surgery and deliver them straight to your door.
  • Blister Packs: Simplify your medication management with our organized blister packs.
  • Pharmacy First: Expert treatment for minor ailments such as sinusitis, sore throat, acute otitis media, infected insect bites, impetigo, shingles, and uncomplicated urinary tract infections (UTIs).
  • Travel Vaccinations: Free assessment for your travel vaccination needs.
  • Non-Travel Vaccinations: Stay protected with our comprehensive vaccination services.
  • Private Clinic: Address over 60 different conditions without the wait to see your GP. Convenient appointments and expert care.
  • Blood Testing: Fast results for your blood tests – when was the last time you had yours checked?
  • Weight Loss Clinic: Innovative solutions that deliver results. Tried the rest? Now try the best.
  • Private Consultation Room: Free advice on your medications in a private and confidential setting.

As a family business, we uphold strong ethics and a dedication to our community. Our knowledgeable staff, safe procedures, and the latest pharmacy technology ensure you receive the best care possible.

Why Choose Gordon Short Chemist?

Choosing a pharmacy is a crucial decision. At Gordon Short Chemist, we treat you as a person, not just a patient. Our positive reviews speak to our exceptional service and the trust our community places in us. With a reputation for excellence, we are proud to be a top-rated pharmacy in Crosby, Liverpool.
