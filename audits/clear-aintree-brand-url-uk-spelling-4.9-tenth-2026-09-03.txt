INJECTION 1 (self-corrected before reporting - see NOTE below).
First attempt: the pack's own heading line "# GBP content pack - Clear
Chemist Aintree" changed to "Clea Chemist Aintree" (dropped the letter
"r"). Result: check-brand-spelling.js exit 0 - NOT caught.

This is not a checker gap. Reading tools/check-brand-spelling.js's rule 2
(wordForms()) before drawing that conclusion: near-miss forms are derived
from the canonical brand only as a trailing "s" added or dropped, an
apostrophe-s form, "and" written as "&", the shop-type word swapped
(Chemist/Chemists/Pharmacy/Pharmacies), and an internal capital flattened
to sentence case. "Clear" has no trailing "s", no "and", no shop-type word
and no internal capital to flatten, so wordForms("Clear") only ever
produces "Clear", "Clears" and "Clear's". Dropping a mid-word letter is
outside every one of those derived families and is not one of the seven
explicitly listed MISSPELT transliterations (MacCann, Hirschman,
Tiffenburg, S K Chemists, Mc Cann, Smarts, Ridings) either. The injection
tested a typo class the rule was never designed to catch, not a hole in
what it does cover - the same "own-method error, caught before reporting"
shape several recent passes (4.13 tenth, 3.11 eighth) have recorded for
themselves. Corrected injection, matching a form the rule actually
derives:

Heading changed to "# GBP content pack - Clears Chemist Aintree" (trailing
s added, one of wordForms("Clear")'s two derived near-miss forms).
Result: CAUGHT.
