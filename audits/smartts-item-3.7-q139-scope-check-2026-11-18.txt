Item 3.7 (Smartts Chemist, Bootle) - Q139 follow-up, scope check
Run 534 (Sonnet 5, Cowork scheduled run, audit-backlog-worker, 2026-11-18)

PURPOSE
Q139 (raised run502, 2026-11-17, still open) found that Smartts Chemist's
live Weebly site carries a standalone AEO/AI-assistant page,
/hey-ai-read-this.html, that names and links www.simpleweightloss.co.uk as
the branch's weight management clinic - a live reference to a brand that is
formally parked pending a 90-day decision. Q139's own recommended option
(option 3 of 3) was to check whether the same page exists on other branches'
Weebly sites before deciding anything, since the scope of the live exposure
could be larger than one branch. That check is read-only research, not a
decision requiring Rishi, so it was carried out this run rather than left
for the open question to sit on.

METHOD (read-only, Claude in Chrome, nothing clicked/typed/submitted)
All 11 distinct live website hosts in branches.json were checked (15 trading
branches share some hosts: Scorah pair, McCanns pair, Fishlocks pair).
Two independent checks per host:
  1. Direct fetch of https://<host>/hey-ai-read-this.html (the exact filename
     Smartts uses).
  2. Fetch of https://<host>/sitemap.xml (or, for Clear Chemist, its sitemap
     index) and a full read of every listed URL, in case another branch
     published the same page under a different filename.

CONCLUSION
The /hey-ai-read-this.html page, and the live simpleweightloss.co.uk
reference inside it, is confined to Smartts Chemist, Bootle. It was not
copy-pasted across the Weebly estate the way the switch-page and eMAR
paste templates were (CLAUDE.md's "public-copy" sections). This narrows
Q139 from a possible estate-wide exposure to a single-branch one. Q139's
own option 3 is now answered; the choice between Q139's remaining two
options (leave as-is pending the SWL decision, or pull/edit the page now)
is still Rishi's to make and Q139 stays open for that.
