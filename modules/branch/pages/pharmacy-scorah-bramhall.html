Questions people usually ask

Do you deliver prescriptions?
Yes. Ask in store or call us and the team will explain how collection and delivery work for Bramhall, Cheadle Hulme, Hazel Grove, Handforth and Poynton.
Can I switch my prescriptions to you?
Yes, and it takes about a minute. Visit our switch your prescriptions page or call us and we will handle the rest with your GP surgery.
Do I need a GP appointment for Pharmacy First?
No. Pharmacy First is a free NHS service where our pharmacist can assess and, where appropriate, treat seven common conditions. See our Pharmacy First page.
