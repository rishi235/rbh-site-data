THE DEFECT FOUND ON THIS PASS, one notch along the run-178 axis
Run 178 established that the town and brand a page carries must be asserted
against branches.json directly, not against seo-pattern pick()'s own answer,
because pick() feeds both the composers and the checks - circular. The rule it
added covers pick().town and pick().brand. pick() returns THREE values. The
third, region, is the landing-title qualifier ("Pharmacy in Eccleston,
Chorley - Fishlocks Chemist"), contracted in pick()'s own comment and
CLAUDE.md as seoRegion where set, else addressRegion. seoRegion exists for
exactly one reason: where the borough beats the county as the local search
word (Eccleston, Chorley vs Eccleston, St Helens). Nothing asserted that
source. Every rule that reads the landing title compares it with
landingTitle(), and landingTitle() takes its region from the same pick().
